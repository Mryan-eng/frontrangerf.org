Personal Projects

Hardware builds, infrastructure, and RF experiments.

ADS-B Receive Stations
Designed and deployed multiple ADS-B receive stations for aviation surveillance. Includes antenna design, RF isolation analysis, and long-term coverage monitoring. Data used in co-siting research and published technical reports.
Equipment: USRP, LNA, collinear arrays | Status: Operational
DMR Hotspot (Pi-Star/MMDVM)
Built and configured personal DMR hotspot on Raspberry Pi running Pi-Star with MMDVM hardware. Debugged WiFi link budget, session keepalive, and network connectivity issues. Registered on BrandMeister network.
Platform: Raspberry Pi + MMDVM | Network: BrandMeister | Status: Operational
Deep Space Exploration Society - 18M Dish
Active participant in hands-on work on the 18-meter research dish at Haswell. Perform feed swaps between 408 MHz pulsar observation and 1420 MHz hydrogen line receive chains. Maintenance and stow operations.
Antenna: 18-meter parabolic | Bands: 408 MHz / 1420 MHz | Role: Operator
UCCS DemoSat — VHF Recovery Link
RF/Communications Lead for NASA-funded COSGC high-altitude balloon mission. Designed and validated VHF APRS recovery link. Full link budget analysis, SNR/PER calculations, antenna modeling. Validated 350+ km operational range.
Mission: UCCS DemoSat | Launch: August 2026 | Range: 350+ km | Funding: NASA/COSGC
