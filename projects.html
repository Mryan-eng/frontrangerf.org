CSST Part 15 FM Radio Station
Colorado Springs School of Technology -- 88 to 108 MHz

A legal Part 15 FM station for Colorado Springs School of Technology. The school has no broadcast infrastructure -- this would be the first. Classic alternative format with engineering and technology talk segments. Requires the bandpass filter build first to clean up the identified 2nd and 4th harmonics from the 500mW transmitter. One of the more interesting projects going right now because it combines RF engineering, broadcast operations, and actually doing something useful for the school.

FM Bandpass Filter
88 to 108 MHz -- UCCS Workshop

Design and construction of a bandpass filter for a 500mW FM transmitter. RTL-SDR analysis identified 2nd and 4th harmonics consistent with a sawtooth oscillator clock. Filter built at UCCS workshop. Prerequisite for the CSST FM station.

PiHPSDR -- Portable

Pi 5 with 5" touchscreen running PiHPSDR. Portable field deployable SDR platform for spectrum monitoring and portable ADS-B work outside the permanent station.

NanoVNA Antenna Measurements
DC to 3 GHz -- SWR, Impedance, S21

NanoVNA measurement campaign to validate the FR-2026-05 co-siting analysis predictions against real hardware. SWR and impedance across frequency, S21 isolation between the two installed antennas. Measured vs. predicted isolation is a publishable result on its own.

Planned -- 2026 to 2027
Hydrogen Line Receiver
1420.405 MHz -- 21cm

First radio astronomy project. RTL-SDR V3 with a dedicated feedhorn and 1420 MHz LNA. Building on the ADS-B station hardware. Target: confirmed hydrogen line detection and galactic structure mapping via Doppler shift analysis.

Stellar Labs 30-2460 Pattern Model
MATLAB R2025b -- 7.375 dBi Simulated

The Stellar Labs 30-2460 4-element FM Yagi does not include a radiation pattern in its documentation. Modeled independently in MATLAB using the array factor derivation and pattern multiplication theorem. First independently derived antenna pattern model for FRRRI.
