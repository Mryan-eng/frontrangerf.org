FRRRI-STATION-001
Dual-Band ADS-B Receive Station
978 / 1090 MHz — Permanent Installation
Permanent two-antenna receive station on metal garage roof south of Fountain, CO. Continuous long-term ADS-B coverage data collection to study Front Range terrain propagation asymmetry. This installation provides empirical data on how mountainous terrain affects signal coverage at aviation frequencies.
Location: Fountain, CO | Frequencies: 978 MHz / 1090 MHz | Status: Under Construction

Co-Siting Analysis of a Dual-Band ADS-B Receive Station
Maxwell to Far-Field Integral Equations
Analytical co-siting study deriving elevation patterns from Maxwell's equations. FlightAware collinear antenna reverse-engineered as N=4 element array. FM broadcast band identified as binding isolation constraint. Extended with HFSS full-wave cross-validation for submission to ARRL QEX magazine.

FR-2026-01
Terrestrial Analog Modeling of VHF Propagation
88.33 Miles — The 88-Mile Challenge
Inaugural FRRRI field study. Lookout Mountain to Pikes Peak International Raceway across 7,000–9,000 ft terrain obstacle. Path loss exceeding 110 dB at 99.5 MHz. Demonstrates extreme terrain diffraction in Front Range geometry. Reviewed by Dr. Paul Wilkinson, SIEEE.
Distance: 88.33 miles | Frequency: 99.5 MHz | Obstacle: 7,000–9,000 ft | Published: 2026
