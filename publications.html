Co-siting Analysis of a Dual-Band ADS-B Receive Station: Elevation Pattern Modeling and Antenna Placement Optimization for a Front Range Terrain Propagation Platform
Smith, M. (AF0FO)
FRRRI Technical Report FR-2026-05 — In preparation for ARRL QEX
In Prep

Full analytical co-siting study for the dual-band ADS-B station. Elevation radiation patterns derived from Maxwell's equations via the far-field monopole integral, extended to the collinear case using the pattern multiplication theorem and array factor. The FlightAware collinear's internal construction was reverse-engineered from the published 28° HPBW specification, yielding N=4 half-wave elements and 6.4 dBi modeled gain. Co-polarized isolation modeled as a function of separation distance and frequency. FM broadcast band identified as the binding constraint at 2.3–2.5m for 20 dB. Includes model limitations, full SI units, and NanoVNA validation plan.

Terrestrial Analog Modeling of VHF Propagation: A Comparative Analysis of the Austin Bluffs and Martian Surface Environments
Smith, M. (AF0FO)
FRRRI Technical Report FR-2026-01 — frontrangerf.org — Reviewed by Dr. Paul Wilkinson, SIEEE
Published

Qualitative VHF propagation study using the Rampart Range as a terrestrial analog for Martian terrain. MATLAB link-budget simulation comparing terrestrial and Martian propagation using the Friis transmission equation, showing a 60.2% range collapse on the Martian surface due to the absence of atmospheric refraction. Also documented as the 88-Mile Challenge: characterization of VHF signal persistence across an 88.33-mile obstructed path from Lookout Mountain, Denver to Pikes Peak International Raceway. Inaugural FRRRI study. Reviewed by Dr. Paul Wilkinson, SIEEE, in association with the Pikes Peak Radio Amateur Association.
