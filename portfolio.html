Background
Matthew Smith

Who I Am

Started FRRRI in 2025 with Christmas money, a RTL-SDR, and a question about VHF propagation over the Rampart Range. Everything since — the Extra class license, the MATLAB antenna modeling, the Maxwell derivations, the ADS-B station — built on that first project.

Heading to Colorado School of Mines for Electrical Engineering. Focus: antennas, computational EM, and RF propagation. Longer term: graduate work in Sweden.

What I Work On

Antenna pattern modeling from first principles. Terrain propagation analysis. ADS-B receive station engineering. RF hardware builds. Signal processing. The work spans from Maxwell derivations on paper to physical antenna installation on a garage roof.

All MATLAB code open source under CC BY 4.0. Reviewed by Dr. Paul Wilkinson, SIEEE.

What I've Built
Technical Report
FR-2026-05 Co-siting Analysis

Full Maxwell derivation → far-field integral → E(φ) pattern formula. N=4 collinear reverse-engineering. Binding isolation constraint identified. In preparation for ARRL QEX.

Technical Report
FR-2026-01 VHF Propagation Study

88-mile terrain propagation study across the Rampart Range. Friis link budget, GIS terrain analysis, field measurements. Reviewed by Dr. Paul Wilkinson, SIEEE.

MATLAB Modeling
Antenna Pattern Simulation

Tram 1411 elevation patterns at 10 frequencies. FlightAware collinear array factor model. Stellar Labs Yagi pattern. All derived from first principles, not lookup tables.

Hardware Build
Permanent ADS-B Station

Two-antenna receive station on a metal garage roof. FlightAware collinear + Tram 1411 discone. Raspberry Pi 4, RTL-SDR V3, dump1090-fa, Tailscale remote access.

CubeSat Review
ClydeGuard RF Communications Review

RF and communications review of a NASA-targeted CubeSat forestry health proposal. Identified link budget, latency, and antenna geometry gaps. AIAA Space Lab collaboration.
