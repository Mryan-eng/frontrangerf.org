Independent RF Research -- Colorado Front Range
Terrain Propagation -- Antenna Theory -- Signal Analysis

The Front Range creates real RF challenges: knife-edge diffraction over mountain terrain, asymmetric coverage geometry, and a hard terrain horizon to the west. FRRRI measures, models, and documents these phenomena.
